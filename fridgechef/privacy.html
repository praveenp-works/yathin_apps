Fridge Chef Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Fridge Chef (website and Windows app) is created by Yathin Pedamallu. This policy explains what happens to your information.

Photos you choose

When you take or upload a fridge photo, it is sent directly from your device to Google's Gemini API to identify ingredients and suggest recipes. Fridge Chef does not store your photos on any server of ours. Google's handling is covered by the Gemini API terms and Google's privacy policy.

Your Gemini API key and settings

If you enter a Gemini API key, it is saved only on your device and is sent only to Google with your requests. Diet and time preferences are also kept only on your device.

Accounts

The sign-up form on the website is a demo. Account details are held in memory for that visit only and are not sent anywhere.

Usage counts

The website and app count visits with an anonymous counter (abacus.jasoncameron.dev). No names, emails, or photos are included.

Children

Fridge Chef does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
