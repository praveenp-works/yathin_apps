Plant Doc Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Plant Doc (website and Windows app) is created by Yathin Pedamallu. This policy explains what happens to your information.

Photos you choose

When you take or upload a plant photo, it is sent directly from your device to Google's Gemini API to check the plant's health. Plant Doc does not store your photos on any server of ours. Google's handling is covered by the Gemini API terms and Google's privacy policy.

Your Gemini API key and plants

Your Gemini API key, saved plants and check history are kept only on your device. The key is sent only to Google with your requests.

Usage counts

The website and app count visits with an anonymous counter (abacus.jasoncameron.dev). No names, emails, or photos are included.

Children

Plant Doc does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
