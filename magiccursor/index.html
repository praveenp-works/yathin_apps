Select anything

Drag a box around any part of your screen (a chart, an error, a photo, a page) and ask about it.

Gemini answers

Detailed answers that type out smoothly, with follow-up questions in the same chat.

Make images

Ask it to generate or edit a picture, then save it to Pictures or copy it.

Talk to it

Voice typing with the mic button, and optional read-answers-out-loud.

Saved chats

Your recent chats are kept so you can reopen them.

Light and dark

Pick the theme that suits you.

Four ways to open it, whatever you're doing.

Click

The small round Gemini button at the top right of your screen.

Shortcut

Press Ctrl + Shift + Space.

Shake

Shake your mouse back and forth 6 times quickly.

Say it

“Hey Cursor” or “Magic Cursor”.

Install in two steps.

01

Download

Download MagicCursor.exe and double-click it

No Python or setup needed. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.

Download MagicCursor.exe

02

First run

Paste your free Gemini key

The first time, it asks for your Gemini API key. Get one free and paste it in. That's it.

Get a free Gemini key

Good to know.

Starts with Windows

Turn this off in the gear menu, or in Windows Settings > Apps > Startup.

Updates itself

It checks for a new version every 6 hours and installs it when the chat is closed. Or tap the gear and choose “Check for updates”.

Privacy

Your key stays on your PC. Screenshots you select are sent to Google Gemini only to answer your question.

Quitting

Open it, tap the gear, tap Quit.

Image generation

May need billing turned on for your Gemini key.
