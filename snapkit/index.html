Snap Kit

Eight handy tools in one free app. Snap a photo, draw, or answer a few questions.

Free · 8 tools · Created by Yathin Pedamallu

🪙 Coin Counter

Spread your coins out, snap them, and get the total.

🛋️ Map My Room

Snap your room and get 3 new furniture layouts as floor plans.

✏️ Doodle to Story

Draw anything. It becomes a short bedtime story starring your doodle.

🎲 Game Referee

Snap the board, ask your rules question, get a fair call.

🚗 Dash Light

Snap a warning light on the dashboard. Find out what it means and how urgent it is.

✂️ Sticker Maker

Snap something on a plain background. It cuts it out as a sticker. No API key needed.

🎁 Gift Finder

Answer 4 quick questions and get 5 gift ideas under your budget.

💧 Water Reminder

Track your glasses today and get a nudge to drink. No API key needed.

How it works

👆Pick a tool
📸Snap, draw, or answer a few questions
✨Gemini gives you the answer in seconds

Six tools use your own free Gemini API key, saved only in this browser. Sticker Maker and Water Reminder work with no key. Dash Light is a helper, not a mechanic: if a light is red or flashing, stop safely and call for help.
