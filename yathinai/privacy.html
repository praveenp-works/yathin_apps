Yathin AI privacy policy

Updated November 14, 2026.

Sign-in. If you sign in with Google, Yathin AI only reads your name, email and profile picture. They are saved in your own browser on this device. We don't keep them on a server and we never sell or share them.

Chats. Your projects are saved in your browser. When the Smart brain is on, your messages are sent to Cloudflare Workers AI (or Pollinations as a backup) to write the answer, and are not stored by Yathin AI.

Email codes. Parent and admin codes are sent through EmailJS to the email you type.

Delete. Clear this site's data in your browser to remove everything.
