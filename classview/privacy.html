ClassView Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

ClassView (website and Windows app) is created by Yathin Pedamallu. This policy explains what happens to your information.

Demo data

ClassView currently shows a demo classroom with made-up students. It does not connect to, watch, or collect anything from real devices.

Accounts

The teacher sign-up form is a demo. Details stay on your device and are not sent to any server of ours.

Usage counts

The website and app count visits with an anonymous counter (abacus.jasoncameron.dev). No names, emails, or photos are included.

Children

ClassView does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
