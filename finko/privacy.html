Virtual Finko Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Virtual Finko (website and Windows app) is created by Yathin Pedamallu. This policy explains what happens to your information.

What Finko keeps

Finko's money, outfit, furniture and other game progress are saved only on your device. Nothing is sent to a server of ours.

Voice and chat

Finko talks using your device's built-in speech voice. Anything you type to Finko stays on your device.

Usage counts

The website and app count visits with an anonymous counter (abacus.jasoncameron.dev). No names, emails, or photos are included.

Children

Virtual Finko does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
