Magic Cursor

Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

Magic Cursor is a free Windows 11 app, created by Yathin Pedamallu. You select part of your screen and ask Google Gemini about it. This page explains what the app does with your information. In short, we don't collect it.

What we collect

Nothing. Magic Cursor has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and it sends nothing to us. We don't sell or share any data.

What goes to Google Gemini

To answer you, the app sends these to Google's Gemini API using your own Gemini API key, and only when you ask something:

  • the part of the screen you selected,
  • your typed or spoken question, and the chat so far,
  • links in your question, which Gemini may open, plus Google Search, which Gemini may use to answer.

Google handles this data under its own terms: the Gemini API Terms and the Google Privacy Policy.

Microphone and voice

If voice is on, the app listens on your PC for "Hey Cursor" or "Magic Cursor". This listening happens offline on your PC. When the app thinks it heard the phrase, a short audio clip, about two seconds long, goes to Gemini to check. Questions you ask with the mic also go to Gemini. You can turn voice off from the floating button's menu, or in Windows Settings > Privacy & security > Microphone.

What stays on your PC

Your Gemini API key, your settings and your chat history are saved only on your PC, in your Windows user folder. Uninstalling the app or deleting that folder removes them.

Updates

The Microsoft Store version gets updates from the Microsoft Store. The version you download from this website checks GitHub for new versions. Neither sends any of your data.

This website

This site keeps a simple anonymous count of visits. It doesn't use cookies, ads or profiles.

Children

Magic Cursor doesn't collect anyone's personal information. Gemini API keys are subject to Google's own age rules.
