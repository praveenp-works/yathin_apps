Plant Doc checks your houseplant from one photo. Snap a picture and Google Gemini gives it a health score from 0 to 100, tells you what's wrong, and gives simple care tips for water, light and soil. Save your plants and watch them get healthier over time. Your Gemini key stays on your device, and photos go only to Gemini. Free for Windows, made by Yathin Pedamallu.
